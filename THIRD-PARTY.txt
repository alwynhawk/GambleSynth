Third-party components in GambleSynth

The synthesis engine in GambleSynth is written from scratch — the oscillators,
filters, envelopes, effects and the randomiser are our own code. What follows is
everything else the software is built on.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Artwork and sound
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The cabinet artwork, the lever, the fruit symbols and the icon were drawn for
this project and are ours.

No sampled audio ships with GambleSynth. Every wavetable is generated at startup
from harmonic recipes in code, so there is no sample library and no sample
licence to observe.
